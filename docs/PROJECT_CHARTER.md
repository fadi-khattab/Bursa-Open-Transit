# BOT — Project charter (M0.1)

## Mission
Make accurate, accessible public transport information easier to find for residents and visitors of Bursa. The initiative is independent, volunteer-driven and non-commercial; it is not a registered nonprofit or an agency representative by default.

## Scope
Initial scope: scheduled bus, tram and BursaRay information, subject to agency agreement. Realtime is a later, separate phase. No ticketing, personal location tracking, or passenger records are required.

## Stakeholders and responsibilities (proposed)
- BURULAŞ: authoritative transport source, data access and explicit authorization, operational correction channel.
- BOT: adapters, transformation, testing, monitoring, documentation and maintenance within agreed scope.
- Google Transit: partner onboarding, acceptance and publication decisions for Google Maps.
- Users: report errors via a future documented channel; do not treat experimental data as journey advice.

## Current status
Google Transit indicated that an independent developer can participate with official agency endorsement. This is conditional eligibility, not approval of an actual feed. No agency endorsement, source access, or publication approval has been established.

## Success criteria
An authorized, validated, current feed; reproducible generation; stable IDs; correction and rollback procedures; named maintenance owner and handover process; agency-approved publication.

## Non-goals
No unauthorized ingestion or redistribution, no use of private endpoints without permission, no impersonation of agency, and no claim of complete network coverage until independently verified.
